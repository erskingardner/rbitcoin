Changed

- **IBD lookup no longer reads parents that are still in the confirm pipeline.** A lookup wave left out of its `tx.head` read only the transactions created in the same wave. A parent created by an earlier wave whose block was not yet connected was read, missed in every sealed `tx.head` segment, and then bound by load from in-flight anyway. Lookup now keeps the txids of the blocks it has decoded above its last drain+fence snapshot and leaves those parents out too. In a control sync, 30% to 50% of the txids lookup read between heights 400,000 and 850,000 were such parents. The set is capped at 900,000 txids (the lowest heights leave first), empties on every re-arm, and binds nothing: load binds these parents from in-flight as before. The set's estimated heap counts toward the 1 GiB accounted heap the in-flight pin window is sized against. `ibd: perf` gains `lookup_undrained_skip` and `conf_pipe.undrained_bytes`. In 20-minute runs at heights 861,000 to 902,000 on a build where the write thread was no longer the limit, such parents were 12% of the keys, and leaving them out took the lookup thread's work from 25.1 ms to 21.8–23.0 ms per block.
