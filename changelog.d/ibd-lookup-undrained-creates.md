Changed

- **IBD lookup no longer reads parents that are still in the confirm pipeline.** A lookup wave left out of its `tx.head` read only the transactions created in the same wave. A parent created by an earlier wave whose block was not yet connected was read, missed in every sealed `tx.head` segment, and then bound by load from in-flight anyway. Lookup now keeps the txids of the blocks it has decoded above its last drain+fence snapshot and leaves those parents out too. In a control sync, 30% to 50% of the txids lookup read between heights 400,000 and 850,000 were such parents. The set is capped at 900,000 txids (the lowest heights leave first), empties on every re-arm, and binds nothing: load binds these parents from in-flight as before. `ibd: perf` gains `lookup_undrained_skip`.
